**Product Requirements Document (PRD)**
=====================================

**1. App Name and Tagline**
-------------------------

* App Name: **LifeOS**
* Tagline: **"Transform Your Life, One Habit at a Time."**

**2. Problem Statement**
----------------------

As a busy individual, Devendra, you struggle to keep track of various aspects of your life, including health, fitness, sleep, nutrition, tasks, finances, and personal growth. You need a comprehensive, private, and personalized app that helps you monitor, analyze, and improve your overall well-being.

**3. Target Users**
-----------------

* **Primary User:** Devendra (the app's sole user)
* **User Characteristics:**
	+ Tech-savvy individual
	+ Health and fitness enthusiast
	+ Busy professional with multiple responsibilities
	+ Values data-driven insights and personalized recommendations
	+ Prioritizes privacy and security

**4. Core Feature List with Priority**
------------------------------------

### P0 (High Priority)

1. **Dashboard**: A personalized dashboard displaying daily scores, health metrics, and key insights.
2. **Health Tracking**: Comprehensive health tracking features, including weight, BMI, body fat, and vital signs.
3. **Workout Tracking**: Gym and workout tracking with exercise library, sets, reps, weight, and rest time.
4. **Food and Nutrition**: Calorie, protein, carb, and fat tracking with meal planning and nutrition insights.
5. **Sleep and Recovery**: Sleep duration, bedtime, wake time, and recovery score tracking.
6. **Tasks and Habits**: Daily todo, weekly goals, monthly goals, and habit tracking with streak system.
7. **Books and Knowledge**: Book tracking, skill tracking, and daily learning plan.
8. **Finance**: Income, expenses, savings, and investment tracking.

### P1 (Medium Priority)

1. **Rewards / Solo Leveling**: XP, levels, badges, achievements, and streaks system.
2. **AI Assistant**: AI-powered recommendations and insights for health, fitness, and nutrition.
3. **Organ Health Pages**: Detailed pages for heart, brain, liver, kidney, lungs, stomach/gut, bones, skin, muscles, blood, and hormones.
4. **Medical Reports**: Storage and tracking of medical reports, lab results, and body photos.

### P2 (Low Priority)

1. **DIY Wearable Integration**: Integration with wearable devices for tracking additional health metrics.
2. **Local LLM**: Integration with local language models for enhanced AI insights.
3. **3D Body Twin**: 3D body modeling for tracking body transformation.

**5. MVP Scope**
----------------

The Minimum Viable Product (MVP) will include the following features:

* Dashboard
* Health tracking
* Workout tracking
* Food and nutrition tracking
* Sleep and recovery tracking
* Tasks and habits tracking
* Books and knowledge tracking
* Finance tracking
* AI recommendation page
* Basic charts and progress cards
* Privacy settings

**6. User Stories**
-----------------

### Dashboard

* As a user, I want to see a personalized dashboard displaying my daily scores, health metrics, and key insights.
* As a user, I want to be able to customize my dashboard to display the metrics that matter most to me.

### Health Tracking

* As a user, I want to track my weight, BMI, body fat, and vital signs.
* As a user, I want to receive alerts and notifications when my health metrics exceed normal ranges.

### Workout Tracking

* As a user, I want to track my workouts, including exercises, sets, reps, weight, and rest time.
* As a user, I want to receive personalized workout recommendations based on my fitness goals and progress.

### Food and Nutrition

* As a user, I want to track my daily calorie, protein, carb, and fat intake.
* As a user, I want to receive personalized nutrition recommendations based on my dietary needs and preferences.

### Sleep and Recovery

* As a user, I want to track my sleep duration, bedtime, wake time, and recovery score.
* As a user, I want to receive alerts and notifications when my sleep and recovery metrics indicate poor sleep quality.

### Tasks and Habits

* As a user, I want to track my daily todo, weekly goals, monthly goals, and habits.
* As a user, I want to receive reminders and notifications to help me stay on track with my goals and habits.

### Books and Knowledge

* As a user, I want to track my reading progress, including books, pages, chapters, and notes.
* As a user, I want to receive personalized book recommendations based on my reading interests and preferences.

### Finance

* As a user, I want to track my income, expenses, savings, and investments.
* As a user, I want to receive alerts and notifications when my financial metrics exceed normal ranges.

**8. Success Metrics**
---------------------

* User engagement (daily active users, retention rate)

**9. Out of Scope**
------------------

* Public SaaS features (sign-up, community, social sharing, subscription)
* Multi-user system
* Integration with wearable devices (DIY wearable integration is a P2 feature)

**10. Risks and Assumptions**
-------------------------

* Technical risks: data storage and security, AI model accuracy, integration with third-party services
* User risks: user adoption and engagement, user satisfaction
* Assumptions: user will provide accurate and consistent data, user will engage with the app regularly
